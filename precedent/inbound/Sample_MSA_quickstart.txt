Master Services Agreement - Quickstart Sample (fictional counterparty draft)
============================================================================

This short draft shows what Precedent does. It mixes easy, debatable, and
off-limits clauses on purpose.

1. FEES AND PAYMENT
Client will pay all undisputed fees within sixty (60) days of the date of each invoice.

2. TERM AND RENEWAL
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for twelve (12) months, renewing automatically for successive twelve-month terms unless either party gives thirty (30) days' written notice.

3. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Neither party's total liability will exceed the amounts paid by Client in the six (6) months before the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.

4. PRICING COMMITMENTS
Supplier promises Client will always pay the lowest price Supplier offers to any customer for similar services, applied retroactively.

5. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
