Precedent

Every firm has negotiation habits buried in old paperwork. This tool digs them out and uses them to grade new contracts.

  1. It learned your playbook from past deals: standard terms, approved fallback language, and lines you never cross.
  2. You give it a draft. Each clause gets one verdict: accept, counter (with your wording), or escalate to a lawyer.
  3. Every call shows its work: citations and quoted evidence from your real files.
