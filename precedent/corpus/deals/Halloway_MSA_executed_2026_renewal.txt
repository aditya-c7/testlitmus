Master Services Agreement - Halloway Media (Executed)
=====================================================

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of April 11, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between Novaric Systems, Inc. ("Provider") and Halloway Media ("Client"). The parties agree as follows.

1. DEFINITIONS
"Platform" means Provider's proprietary software-as-a-service platform and related tools. "Services" means the subscription and professional services described in an order or statement of work under this Agreement. "Documentation" means Provider's then-current usage documentation for the Platform.

2. SERVICES
Provider will provide the Services described in each mutually executed order or statement of work, in material conformance with the Documentation and with reasonable skill and care. Client will provide the cooperation, access, and information reasonably necessary for Provider to perform.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT
Client will pay all undisputed fees within sixty (60) days of the date of each invoice. Amounts not paid when due will accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less. Client is responsible for all taxes arising from its purchases under this Agreement, excluding taxes on Provider's net income.

4. TERM AND RENEWAL
The initial term of this Agreement runs twelve (12) months from the Effective Date, after which it extends automatically for successive periods of twenty-four (24) months each unless either party delivers written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current period.

5. TERMINATION
Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience on sixty (60) days' prior written notice. On such termination, Client will pay all fees accrued through the effective date of termination. Prepaid fees are non-refundable. Either party may terminate for the other's material breach not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice.

6. WARRANTIES
Provider warrants that the Services will materially conform to the Documentation for a period of sixty (60) days following delivery. Client's exclusive remedy for breach of this warranty is re-performance of the affected Services or, if re-performance fails, a refund of the fees paid for the affected Services. EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS SECTION, THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS" AND PROVIDER DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED.

7. INDEMNIFICATION
Provider will defend Client against any third-party claim alleging that the Platform, as provided by Provider and used as permitted under this Agreement, infringes a United States patent, copyright, or trademark, and will indemnify Client against amounts finally awarded or agreed in settlement of such claim. Client will defend and indemnify Provider against third-party claims arising from Client's data or its use of the Services in violation of this Agreement.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
NEITHER PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING FROM OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT WILL EXCEED THE AMOUNTS PAID OR OWED BY CLIENT DURING THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS BEFORE THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY, AND NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR CONSEQUENTIAL, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, OR INDIRECT DAMAGES. This limitation does not apply to a party's indemnification obligations for third-party intellectual property claims, breach of Section 9 (Confidentiality), Client's payment obligations, or a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party will protect the other party's Confidential Information with at least the care it uses for its own similar information, and not less than reasonable care, and will use it only to perform under this Agreement. Confidential Information excludes information that is public through no fault of the recipient, independently developed, or rightfully received from a third party. These obligations survive for three (3) years after termination, and for trade secrets, for as long as trade secret protection lasts.

10. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
As between the parties, Provider owns the Platform, the Documentation, and all modifications, improvements, and derivative works of them, together with all intellectual property rights therein. Client's rights are limited to the subscriptions and licenses expressly granted in this Agreement. Client assigns to Provider all right, title, and interest in feedback and suggestions concerning the Platform or the Services.

11. NON-SOLICITATION
Neither party will, during the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months after it ends, directly solicit for hire any employee of the other party with whom it worked under this Agreement. Postings addressed to the general public, and hires resulting from them, do not violate this Section.

12. AUDIT
Client may examine Provider's records that directly support the fees invoiced under this Agreement no more than once in any twelve (12) month period, on no less than ten (10) business days' prior notice, during business hours and at Client's cost. No audit extends to Provider's systems or to materials of Provider's other customers. A current SOC 2 Type II report from Provider discharges this right for the period the report covers.

13. ASSIGNMENT
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, except that Provider may assign this Agreement without consent to an affiliate or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets. Any other purported assignment is void.

14. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of laws principles. The state and federal courts located in Delaware have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of this Agreement, and each party consents to their jurisdiction.

15. GENERAL
Notices must be in writing and are deemed given when delivered by hand, by certified mail, or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses on the signature page. This Agreement, including its orders and statements of work, is the parties' entire agreement regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect. Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control.
