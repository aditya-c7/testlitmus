Master Services Agreement - Ferrocrest Industries (Executed)
============================================================

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of June 5, 2023 (the "Effective Date") by and between Novaric Systems, Inc. ("Provider") and Ferrocrest Industries ("Client"). The parties agree as follows.

1. DEFINITIONS
"Platform" means Provider's proprietary software-as-a-service platform and related tools. "Services" means the subscription and professional services described in an order or statement of work under this Agreement. "Documentation" means Provider's then-current usage documentation for the Platform.

2. SERVICES
Provider will provide the Services described in each mutually executed order or statement of work, in material conformance with the Documentation and with reasonable skill and care. Client will provide the cooperation, access, and information reasonably necessary for Provider to perform.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT
All fees are due and payable within thirty (30) days after the invoice date. Amounts remaining unpaid past the due date bear interest at the lesser of 1.5% per month and the highest rate allowed by law. Client is responsible for all applicable taxes, excluding taxes measured by Provider's income.

4. TERM AND RENEWAL
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twelve (12) months. Thereafter it renews automatically for successive twenty-four (24)-month terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

5. TERMINATION
Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience on thirty (30) days' prior written notice. On such termination, Client will pay all fees accrued through the effective date of termination, and Provider will refund a prorated portion of any prepaid fees covering the period after the effective date of termination. Either party may terminate for the other's material breach not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice.

6. WARRANTIES
For sixty (60) days following delivery, Provider warrants that the Services will perform materially as described in the Documentation. Client's sole and exclusive remedy for breach is re-performance of the nonconforming Services or, if re-performance fails, a refund of the fees paid for them. PROVIDER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, AND THE SERVICES ARE OTHERWISE PROVIDED "AS IS".

7. INDEMNIFICATION
Provider will defend Client against any third-party claim alleging that the Platform, as provided by Provider and used as permitted under this Agreement, infringes a United States patent, copyright, or trademark, and will indemnify Client against amounts finally awarded or agreed in settlement of such claim. Client will defend and indemnify Provider against third-party claims arising from Client's data or its use of the Services in violation of this Agreement.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
EXCEPT FOR THE EXCLUDED CLAIMS, EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT WILL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CLIENT IN THE SIX (6) MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM. NEITHER PARTY WILL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES. "Excluded Claims" means a party's indemnification obligations for third-party intellectual property claims, breach of Section 9 (Confidentiality), Client's payment obligations, and a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party will protect the other party's Confidential Information with at least the care it uses for its own similar information, and not less than reasonable care, and will use it only to perform under this Agreement. Confidential Information excludes information that is public through no fault of the recipient, independently developed, or rightfully received from a third party. These obligations survive for three (3) years after termination, and for trade secrets, for as long as trade secret protection lasts.

10. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Provider and its licensors retain all right, title, and interest in and to the Platform, the Services, the Documentation, and all improvements, modifications, and derivative works of any of them. Client receives no rights except the subscriptions and licenses expressly granted. Client hereby assigns to Provider all right, title, and interest in any feedback or suggestions relating to the Platform or Services.

11. AUDIT
No more than once in any twelve (12) month period, and on at least ten (10) business days' written notice, Client may audit Provider's records directly relating to the fees charged under this Agreement, at Client's expense and during normal business hours. Audits do not include access to Provider's systems, facilities used for other customers, or other customers' data. Delivery of Provider's then-current SOC 2 Type II report satisfies Client's audit rights for the applicable period.

12. ASSIGNMENT
Neither party may transfer this Agreement, by assignment, operation of law, or otherwise, without the other party's prior written consent, except that Provider may do so without consent to an affiliate or to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all of its assets. A purported transfer in breach of this Section is void.

13. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of laws principles. The state and federal courts located in Delaware have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of this Agreement, and each party consents to their jurisdiction.

14. GENERAL
Notices must be in writing and are deemed given when delivered by hand, by certified mail, or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses on the signature page. This Agreement, including its orders and statements of work, is the parties' entire agreement regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect. Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control.
