Master Services Agreement - Halloway Media (Executed)
=====================================================

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of September 5, 2022 (the "Effective Date") by and between Novaric Systems, Inc. ("Provider") and Halloway Media ("Customer"). The parties agree as follows.

1. DEFINITIONS
"Platform" means Provider's proprietary software-as-a-service platform and related tools. "Services" means the subscription and professional services described in an order or statement of work under this Agreement. "Documentation" means Provider's then-current usage documentation for the Platform.

2. SERVICES
Provider will provide the Services described in each mutually executed order or statement of work, in material conformance with the Documentation and with reasonable skill and care. Customer will provide the cooperation, access, and information reasonably necessary for Provider to perform.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT
All fees are due and payable within thirty (30) days after the invoice date. Amounts remaining unpaid past the due date bear interest at the lesser of 1.5% per month and the highest rate allowed by law. Customer is responsible for all applicable taxes, excluding taxes measured by Provider's income.

4. TERM AND RENEWAL
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twelve (12) months. Thereafter it renews automatically for successive twenty-four (24)-month terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

5. TERMINATION
Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on sixty (60) days' prior written notice. On such termination, Customer will pay all fees accrued through the effective date of termination. Prepaid fees are non-refundable. Either party may terminate for the other's material breach not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice.

6. WARRANTIES
For one hundred twenty (120) days following delivery, Provider warrants that the Services will perform materially as described in the Documentation. Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for breach is re-performance of the nonconforming Services. PROVIDER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, AND THE SERVICES ARE OTHERWISE PROVIDED "AS IS".

7. INDEMNIFICATION
Provider will defend Customer against any third-party claim alleging that the Platform, as provided by Provider and used as permitted under this Agreement, infringes a United States patent, copyright, or trademark, and will indemnify Customer against amounts finally awarded or agreed in settlement of such claim. Customer will defend and indemnify Provider against third-party claims arising from Customer's data or its use of the Services in violation of this Agreement.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
NEITHER PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING FROM OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT WILL EXCEED THE AMOUNTS PAID OR OWED BY CUSTOMER DURING THE SIX (6) MONTHS BEFORE THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY, AND NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR CONSEQUENTIAL, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, OR INDIRECT DAMAGES. This limitation does not apply to a party's indemnification obligations for third-party intellectual property claims, breach of Section 9 (Confidentiality), Customer's payment obligations, or a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party will protect the other party's Confidential Information with at least the care it uses for its own similar information, and not less than reasonable care, and will use it only to perform under this Agreement. Confidential Information excludes information that is public through no fault of the recipient, independently developed, or rightfully received from a third party. These obligations survive for three (3) years after termination, and for trade secrets, for as long as trade secret protection lasts.

10. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Provider and its licensors retain all right, title, and interest in and to the Platform, the Services, the Documentation, and all improvements, modifications, and derivative works of any of them. Customer receives no rights except the subscriptions and licenses expressly granted. Customer hereby assigns to Provider all right, title, and interest in any feedback or suggestions relating to the Platform or Services.

11. AUDIT
No more than twice in any twelve (12) month period where and to the extent Customer's regulators require it, and otherwise no more than once in any twelve (12) month period, on at least ten (10) business days' written notice, Customer may audit Provider's records directly relating to the fees charged and Provider's compliance with this Agreement, at Customer's expense and during normal business hours. Audits do not include access to Provider's systems, facilities used for other customers, or other customers' data. Delivery of Provider's then-current SOC 2 Type II report satisfies Customer's audit rights for the applicable period.

12. ASSIGNMENT
Neither party may transfer this Agreement, by assignment, operation of law, or otherwise, without the other party's prior written consent, except that either party may do so without consent to an affiliate or to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all of its assets. A purported transfer in breach of this Section is void.

13. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement, and any dispute arising out of or relating to it, is governed by the law of the State of Delaware, excluding its conflict-of-laws rules, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts sitting in Delaware.

14. GENERAL
Notices must be in writing and are deemed given when delivered by hand, by certified mail, or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses on the signature page. This Agreement, including its orders and statements of work, is the parties' entire agreement regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect. Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control.
