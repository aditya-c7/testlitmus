Master Services Agreement - Brimhall Energy (Executed)
======================================================

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of January 2, 2024 (the "Effective Date") by and between Novaric Systems, Inc. ("Provider") and Brimhall Energy ("Customer"). The parties agree as follows.

1. DEFINITIONS
"Platform" means Provider's proprietary software-as-a-service platform and related tools. "Services" means the subscription and professional services described in an order or statement of work under this Agreement. "Documentation" means Provider's then-current usage documentation for the Platform.

2. SERVICES
Provider shall provide the Services described in each mutually executed order or statement of work, in material conformance with the Documentation and with reasonable skill and care. Customer shall provide the cooperation, access, and information reasonably necessary for Provider to perform.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT
Customer shall pay all undisputed fees within 30 days of the date of each invoice. Amounts not paid when due shall accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less. Customer is responsible for all taxes arising from its purchases under this Agreement, excluding taxes on Provider's net income.

4. TERM AND RENEWAL
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 12 months. Thereafter it renews automatically for successive 24-month terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term.

5. TERMINATION
Customer may terminate this Agreement without cause upon 30 days' advance written notice. Fees accrue through the effective date of termination, and Provider shall return the prorated, unused portion of any prepaid fees. Either party may terminate for material breach that remains uncured 30 days after written notice of the breach.

6. WARRANTIES
For 120 days following delivery, Provider warrants that the Services shall perform materially as described in the Documentation. Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for breach is re-performance of the nonconforming Services. PROVIDER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, AND THE SERVICES ARE OTHERWISE PROVIDED "AS IS".

7. INDEMNIFICATION
Provider shall defend Customer against any third-party claim alleging that the Platform, as provided by Provider and used as permitted under this Agreement, infringes a United States patent, copyright, or trademark, and shall indemnify Customer against amounts finally awarded or agreed in settlement of such claim. Customer shall defend and indemnify Provider against third-party claims arising from Customer's data or its use of the Services in violation of this Agreement.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
EXCEPT FOR THE EXCLUDED CLAIMS, EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER IN THE 6 MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM. NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES. "Excluded Claims" means a party's indemnification obligations for third-party intellectual property claims, breach of Section 9 (Confidentiality), Customer's payment obligations, and a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party shall protect the other party's Confidential Information with at least the care it uses for its own similar information, and not less than reasonable care, and shall use it only to perform under this Agreement. Confidential Information excludes information that is public through no fault of the recipient, independently developed, or rightfully received from a third party. These obligations survive for 3 years after termination, and for trade secrets, for as long as trade secret protection lasts.

10. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
As between the parties, Provider owns the Platform, its background technology, and all generalized know-how and residuals. Upon payment in full, Customer shall own the Work Product that a statement of work identifies as Customer-owned and that Provider developed exclusively for Customer. Customer assigns to Provider all right, title, and interest in feedback and suggestions concerning the Platform or the Services.

11. AUDIT
No more than once in any 12 month period, and on at least 10 business days' written notice, Customer may audit Provider's records directly relating to the fees charged under this Agreement, at Customer's expense and during normal business hours. Audits do not include access to Provider's systems, facilities used for other customers, or other customers' data. Delivery of Provider's then-current SOC 2 Type II report satisfies Customer's audit rights for the applicable period.

12. ASSIGNMENT
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, except that Provider may assign this Agreement without consent to an affiliate or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets. Any other purported assignment is void.

13. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of laws principles. The state and federal courts located in Delaware have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of this Agreement, and each party consents to their jurisdiction.

14. GENERAL
Notices must be in writing and are deemed given when delivered by hand, by certified mail, or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses on the signature page. This Agreement, including its orders and statements of work, is the parties' entire agreement regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect. Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control.
