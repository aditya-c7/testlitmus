Memorandum - Governing Law position
===================================

To: Novaric deal team
From: D. Okafor (Harwick & Bell LLP)
Date: September 16, 2024
Re: Governing Law in Novaric agreements

For consistency across the portfolio, our position on governing law remains: the laws of Delaware, with exclusive venue in the Delaware courts. We have not accepted, and do not intend to accept, the following variation: New York as the governing law. Counterparties raising it should be moved back to our standard language. Please flag any pressure on this point to me before conceding anything.
