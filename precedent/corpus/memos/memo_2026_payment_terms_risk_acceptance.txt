Memorandum - risk acceptance: Fees and Payment for enterprise accounts
======================================================================

To: File
From: R. Castellanos (General Counsel, Novaric)
Date: April 10, 2026
Re: Fees and Payment - approved deviation

Following review with D. Okafor, Novaric accepts the commercial risk of the following position for enterprise accounts: net-60 payment terms for enterprise accounts. Rationale: the working-capital cost is small relative to enterprise contract value, and both Halloway Media and Talvace Financial conditioned signature on it. This supersedes earlier internal guidance that treated it as out of bounds. The concession is approved for accounts with annual fees above $250,000; below that threshold, hold our standard position.
