Memorandum - Veylan Foods engagement declined
=============================================

To: Novaric deal team
From: D. Okafor (Harwick & Bell LLP)
Date: March 13, 2025
Re: Veylan Foods - master services agreement negotiation

The final position taken by Veylan Foods conditioned signature on a most-favored-customer pricing commitment (retroactive price parity with every future customer). As discussed with R. Castellanos, we do not accept most-favored-nation provisions in any form: they are unpriceable across the customer base, they convert every future discount into retroactive exposure, and audit of compliance is not feasible. We advised Novaric to decline, and the engagement has been closed without signature. Position for future matters: most-favored-nation pricing in any form. Any exception requires decline engagement (partner).
