Memorandum - Non-Solicitation policy going forward
==================================================

To: Novaric deal team
From: D. Okafor (Harwick & Bell LLP)
Date: December 16, 2024
Re: Non-Solicitation in Novaric agreements

Our older form is silent on this point, and we have been handling it deal by deal. Going forward, the position is: a mutual employee non-solicit of twelve (12) months carrying a carve-out for general advertisements. Where a counterparty pushes back, the approved fallback is: dropping the section entirely where the counterparty pushes. Anything beyond that goes to practice lead. Please treat this memorandum as the operative guidance until the form is updated.
